# Source of truth

Current owner instructions govern intent. The owner's decisions of 2026-10-09 (five decisions ND-1 to ND-5 with amendments, the product AI providers and caps, the continuous-deployment policy) are recorded privately outside this repository and applied in `BUILD-BASELINE.md`. Historical proposals, drafts and assistant suggestions in the owner's private material are not decisions and are not reproduced here.

Runtime truth comes from committed SHA, CI, migration state and observed deployment. A merged PR proves implementation, not deployment. `CURRENT-WORK.md` records what was actually run; `LIVE-ENVIRONMENT-FACTS.md` records what was observed live, by digest.

A disagreement between intent and observation is recorded as SOURCE EXPECTATION / LIVE FACT / CONFLICT / RESOLUTION before product truth is changed. POII itself applies the same rule to its own records: intent and observed reality can differ, and both are shown.
